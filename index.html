04. Featured Projects

Featured | Hackathon Winner Project | 3D Virtual Lab

Water Analysis – Determination of Chemical Parameters

Water Analysis is a browser-based 3D virtual chemistry laboratory for simulating water quality testing experiments. It helps students safely practice titration-based analysis for Total Hardness, Alkalinity, and COD through guided procedures, observation tables, endpoint detection, and validation feedback.

• Problem Solved

Traditional chemistry labs require physical reagents, glassware, safety supervision, and repeated lab access. This project provides a safe, interactive, and repeatable virtual lab experience for learning chemical parameter analysis.
